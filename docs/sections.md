## Contest-critical sections

1. **Ticker** — must scroll village news  
2. **Features highlight cards** — ticker, calendar, voice called out  
3. **Voice** — working demo (language select + tap to speak)  
4. **Calendar** — upcoming local fairs/events  

## Feed vs full social app

The Feed is a **UI demonstration**: sample posts, filters, Support/Save, and an in-browser composer. Refresh clears user-added posts. That is enough for judges to understand the product surface without shipping a backend.
