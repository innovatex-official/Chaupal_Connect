Our Story

The Banyan Tree, Reimagined for Today

For generations, villagers have gathered under the banyan tree to share stories, solve problems, and stay connected. Chaupal Connect brings that timeless tradition into the digital age.

Report a broken streetlight, celebrate a neighbour's achievement, or find the next village fair — this is your community's meeting place, open to everyone.

  • Connect with every household in your village
  • Discuss local issues and find community solutions
  • Stay updated with village news and announcements
